The artist behind the songs

His musical journey

Arijit Singh is an Indian playback singer whose recordings have become central to modern Hindi film music.

Early life and beginnings

Born in Jiaganj, West Bengal, in 1987, Arijit developed an interest in music early in life and received musical training while growing up.

He gained wider attention after appearing on the television singing competition Fame Gurukul in 2005. He later worked in the music industry before becoming a widely recognized playback singer.

Early training

His foundation included exposure to Indian classical music and a strong commitment to practice.

Breakthrough

His rendition of “Tum Hi Ho” from Aashiqui 2 (2013) became a defining hit.

Continued success

He has recorded numerous popular songs for Indian films, reaching audiences across generations.
